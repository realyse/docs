## Download a property report

Open a property and click **Download PDF**. Pulse prepares the report and lets you preview it before you download.

On Enterprise plans, **Download PDF** offers two reports:

- **Full Report**
- **Rental Report**

You can see an example on the Pulse home page under **See sample report**.

## Market reports for a property

With a property open, choose **Market Reports**, then **Sales** or **Rental**, to see how the local market is moving.

## Market Intelligence

Open **Market Intelligence** in the sidebar for wider trends:

- **Sales:** sales price indices, including year-on-year and month-on-month changes and prices by property type
- **Rents:** rental price indices
- **Macro Economics:** the wider economic picture

You can also browse free market trends by postcode district on the public [Market Trends](https://pulse.realyse.com/market-trends) pages.

## Investment Search

**Investment Search** helps you find opportunities, with tools including **Listings**, **Benchmarks** and **EPC Upgrade**. What's available depends on your plan.

## Agent Comparison

**Agent Comparison** shows how estate agents compare for **Sales** and **Rents**, either across an area from the sidebar or for a specific property.
