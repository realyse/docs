## Create an account

1. Go to [pulse.realyse.com](https://pulse.realyse.com) and choose a plan from **Pricing**, or start a valuation from the home page.
2. On **Create an account**, enter your email and click **Sign up**. You can also sign up with **Google** or **Microsoft**.
3. Check your inbox for a confirmation email and click the link to activate your account. If it hasn't arrived after a few minutes, look in your spam folder.
4. On **Complete your profile**, tell us a little about yourself, create a password and click **Continue**.

## Sign in

Go to [pulse.realyse.com](https://pulse.realyse.com) and click **Login**. On the **Welcome back** page, enter your email and password and click **Sign in**, or choose **Google** or **Microsoft** under **or continue with**.

## Reset your password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address on **Reset your password**.
3. Open the link in the email we send you, enter your new password twice and click **Continue**.

## Value your first property

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search for the address">
    Click **New valuation** and type the address into **Search for any UK address**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us your interest">
    Choose whether it's your property, one you're looking to buy, or whether you're just browsing, then click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the valuation">
    Pulse generates the valuation in a few moments. You'll see the estimated value, how confident the estimate is, and a rental estimate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the details">
    If anything about the property is wrong, such as the number of bedrooms or floor area, edit it so the estimate reflects the real property.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The bar at the top of the app shows how many valuations you have left.
