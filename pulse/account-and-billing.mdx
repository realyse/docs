Open **Settings** from the menu at the bottom of the sidebar to manage your profile and subscription.

## Plans

Pulse has subscription plans, including **Basic**, **Advanced** and **Enterprise**, and one-off options if you only need a single report. Plans differ in how many valuation reports you get and which tools you can use, such as **Property Oracle**, **Investment Search** and portfolio tracking.

See the **Pricing** page on [pulse.realyse.com](https://pulse.realyse.com) for current plans and prices. For an Enterprise plan, contact REalyse.

## Upgrade your plan

Click **Upgrade plan** in the sidebar menu and choose a plan.

## Buy valuation credits

If you've used your valuations, click **Buy credits** in the sidebar menu, or **Buy valuation credits** in **Settings**. Some credit packs expire after a set period, which is shown before you buy.

## Update your payment method

Go to **Settings**, then **Subscription**. Under **Billing**, click **Update payment method**. This opens our secure payment portal, where you can change your card details.

## Cancel your subscription

Go to **Settings**, then **Subscription**, and click **Cancel subscription**. Follow the steps to confirm.

## Sign out

Click **Log out** in the sidebar menu.

## Get help

- Click **Feedback** in the sidebar menu to send us a message from inside the app.
- Email [support@realyse.com](mailto:support@realyse.com).
