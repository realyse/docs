REalyse Pulse gives you an instant valuation and rental estimate for any UK property, along with the comparables, listings and local market data behind it. It's built for homeowners, buyers, sellers, landlords, investors, agents and mortgage brokers.

## What you can do

- Value any UK address and see how confident the estimate is
- Get a rental estimate and gross yield
- Review comparable sales and current listings nearby
- See local demographics, risk, schools and amenities
- Download a PDF report for a property
- Track several properties together in a portfolio
- Follow sales and rental price trends in **Market Intelligence**
- Set alerts on properties and portfolios

Some features depend on your plan. See [Account and billing](/pulse/account-and-billing).

## Find your way around

The sidebar in the Pulse app includes:

| Menu item | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Your starting point |
| **Valuations** | Every property you've valued |
| **Portfolios** | Groups of properties you track together |
| **Alerts** | Alerts on your properties and portfolios |
| **Market Intelligence** | Sales and rental price indices and wider economic data |
| **Investment Search** | Listings, benchmarks and other tools for finding opportunities |
| **Agent Comparison** | How estate agents compare for sales and rents |
| **Property Oracle** | Ask questions about property in plain English |
| **Renovations AI** | Explore how renovations could look and what they could add |

The menu at the bottom of the sidebar has **Buy credits**, **Upgrade plan**, **Feedback**, **Settings** and **Log out**.
