## Value a property

Click **New valuation** and search for the address in **Search for any UK address**. Pulse works out the property from the address, so you don't need to know its UPRN or title number.

Every property you've valued appears under **Valuations** in the sidebar.

## What a valuation shows

| Figure | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Our Valuation Estimate** | Our estimate of what the property is worth today |
| **Estimate Confidence Level** | How close we expect the estimate to be (see below) |
| **Our rental estimate** | What the property could rent for |
| **Gross Yield** | Annual rental estimate as a percentage of the valuation |
| **Asset Return (Last 5 Years)** | How the property's value has changed over the last five years |
| **Cap Rate** | Net income as a percentage of the valuation |

Some properties are also marked as **Top 15% Premium Properties**.

### Confidence levels

| Level | Expected accuracy |
| --- | --- |
| **High** | Within 1 to 5% of the actual price |
| **Fair** | Within 5 to 10% |
| **Low** | Within 10 to 15% |

Confidence is usually lower for unusual properties, or where there are few recent sales nearby.

## Explore a property

When a valuation is open, the property menu has more detail:

- **Details:** bedrooms, property type, floor area, price per square foot, EPC rating, tenure, last sale and more
- **Charts:** how values have moved over time
- **Demographics & Risk** and **Schools & Amenities:** what the area is like
- **Market Reports:** sales and rental market reports for the area
- **Comparables** and **Listings:** similar properties that have sold, and those for sale or rent now
- **Agent Comparison:** how local estate agents compare
- **District Map:** the property in its wider area

## Correct the property details

Our valuation is only as good as the details we hold. If something is wrong, open the property, edit **Property Details** and update fields such as **Property Type**, **Number of Bedrooms**, **Size (sq ft)**, **Tenure** or **Years Remaining on Lease**. Leave a field empty if you're not sure.

## Refresh a valuation

To get an up-to-date figure, click **Refresh valuation** on the property and confirm. The property is unavailable for about a minute while Pulse recalculates.

## Valuation credits

Each new valuation uses one of your valuation credits. The bar at the top of the app shows how many you have left. When you run out, you can **Buy valuation credits** or upgrade your plan. See [Account and billing](/pulse/account-and-billing).

## Portfolios and alerts

- Use **Portfolios** to track several properties together.
- Use **Alerts** to keep an eye on individual properties or whole portfolios.
