REalyse MCP connects AI assistants to REalyse data through the [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) (MCP). Once it's connected, you can ask your assistant about the UK property market in plain English and it looks up the answer in REalyse.

You sign in with your existing REalyse account. You don't need a separate login, and you don't need to paste tokens or API keys into your assistant.

## What you can ask about

- Sold prices, asking prices and asking rents for individual properties
- Monthly market trends, including price per square foot, rents, yields, transactions and days on market
- Planning applications and the development pipeline for an area
- HM Land Registry titles, ownership and land transactions
- Energy Performance Certificates (EPCs)
- Demographics, crime and nearby amenities
- Policy rates: Local Housing Allowance, London Living Rent and the Community Infrastructure Levy

For example, you could ask:

- "What have two-bed flats sold for within a mile of Clapham Junction this year?"
- "Show me the trend in achieved rents in Manchester city centre over the last three years."
- "Which residential planning applications over 50 units have been approved in Leeds since January?"

[Available tools](/mcp/tools) lists everything your assistant can look up.

## Supported assistants

REalyse MCP works with any MCP client that supports MCP OAuth sign-in. That includes Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and Microsoft Copilot Studio.

## Before you start

MCP access has to be enabled on your REalyse account. If you aren't sure whether you have it, ask your REalyse account manager.

Each account has a monthly data allowance, measured in rows of data returned. [Usage limits](/mcp/usage-limits) explains how it's counted.
