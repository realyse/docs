Add results from **Explore** to the project you have open. Those tiles are the dashboard for that project.

## Add a location

On the map, use the box labelled **Search for a place**. Choose a suggestion, such as a postcode.

<Frame caption="A postcode selected on the map.">
  <img src="/images/core/add-location.png" alt="Map with London SW1A 1AA selected and the Locations control" />
</Frame>

The place stays on the map. Open **Locations** to use it again in this project.

## Open Comparables

Click **Explore** in the sidebar and choose **Comparables**.

The **Location** field should already show the area you added. If it does not, open **Location** and select it.

<Frame caption="Comparables with a location selected, before Apply.">
  <img src="/images/core/comparables-apply.png" alt="Comparables panel with Location set to London and the Apply button" />
</Frame>

## Click Apply

Click **Apply**. The panel loads the matching records, and **Add to Dashboard** becomes available.

<Frame caption="Add to Dashboard is available after Apply.">
  <img src="/images/core/add-to-dashboard.png" alt="Comparables panel after Apply, with the Add to Dashboard button" />
</Frame>

## Add to Dashboard

Click **Add to Dashboard**.

The **Add To Dashboard** dialog asks you to choose a **Section**. Select one, such as **General**, or choose **Create New Section** and type a name. Then click **Add To Dashboard**.

<Frame caption="Choose the section for the new tile.">
  <img src="/images/core/add-to-dashboard-section.png" alt="Add To Dashboard dialog with the General section" />
</Frame>

The same **Apply** and **Add to Dashboard** buttons are on the other Explore datasets, such as **Planning**.

For the datasets themselves, see [Data and locations](/core/data-and-locations). For how tiles are organised in a project, see [Projects and templates](/core/projects).
