The **Site Board** is where you keep land titles you want to come back to. Open it from **Site Board** in the left sidebar.

A new site is added to the first column. On a new board, that column is named **Shortlisted Sites**.

## Add a site

1. Open a project and select **Land & Ownership**.
2. Open **Matching records**.
3. On the land title, click the purple plus icon. Its tooltip is **Add to ...**.
4. Choose **Add to Site Board**.

</Frame>

The app confirms with **Site was successfully added to board**.

## Find the site

Open **Site Board** in the left sidebar. The title is a card in the first column, showing its title number, tenure and **Show full info**.

<Frame caption="The new site card in Shortlisted Sites.">
  <img
    src="/images/site-added-on-board.webp"
    alt="Site Board with a land title card in the Shortlisted Sites column"
  />
</Frame>

If that title is already on the board, the app says **Site is already on the board and cannot be added twice**.

**Site Board** is included on some plans. When it is not included, the sidebar icon is locked.

To download the board, see [Export your work](/core/exports).
