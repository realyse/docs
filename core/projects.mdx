A project is a page of tiles. Each tile shows one view of REalyse data, such as a map of planning applications or a chart of asking rents, for the location and dates you set.

## Tiles and visualisations

When you add data from **Explore**, you choose how to display it. Depending on the dataset, you can choose from:

| Visualisation | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| **Map** | Seeing where records are, such as sales or planning applications |
| **Line** | Trends over time |
| **Bar** | Comparing values across categories or areas |
| **Table** and **Records** | Browsing individual records |
| **Numbers** | Headline figures |
| **Overview** and **Overview Matrix** | Summaries of an area |
| **Affordability** | Affordability measures for an area |
| **Market share for agents** | Estate agent market share |
| **Policy** | Policy rates for an area |
| **Text** | Your own notes and headings |

Not every visualisation works with every dataset. Core only offers the ones that suit the data you've picked.

## Manage your projects

Open **All Projects** in the sidebar to see every project you've created.

- **Group projects:** click **Add New Group**, give it a **Group Name**, then use **Move** on a project card to put it in the group. Projects you haven't grouped appear under **Ungrouped Projects**.
- **Copy, rename or delete:** each project card has **Duplicate**, **Rename** and **Delete**.
- **Recover a deleted project:** deleted projects go to **Deleted Projects**, where you can **Restore** them or **Delete Permanently**.

Inside a project, the project menu also has **Add Notes**, **Clone**, **Save as template** and **Export**.

## Templates

Templates are ready-made project layouts. Open **Templates** in the sidebar to browse three collections:

- **Global Templates**, provided by REalyse
- **Team Templates**, shared by people in your team
- **Personal Templates**, which only you can see

To turn a project into a template, open the project menu and choose **Save as template**. You can save it to **Personal Templates** or share it with your team in **Team Templates**.

Core doesn't have a share button for projects. To give colleagues the same layout, save it as a team template.
