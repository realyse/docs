REalyse Core is a web platform for researching UK property markets. You build **projects**: pages of charts, maps and tables that each show a slice of REalyse data for the locations and dates you choose.

## What you can do

- Look up sold and asking prices, rents and land transactions with **Comparables**
- Track planning applications and the development pipeline with **Planning**
- Find HM Land Registry titles and owners with **Land & Ownership**
- Profile an area with **Demographics**, including crime and broadband, and with **Points of Interest**
- Follow market trends over time with **Markets**
- Check policy rates and local planning policy with **Policies**
- Compare estate agents' market share with **Agent Comparison**
- Get alerts about new and changed planning applications
- Export projects to Word, CSV and Excel, and individual tiles as images

## Find your way around

The sidebar on the left of the app uses icons. Hover over an icon to see its name.

| Icon name | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Explore** | The data panel, where you choose a dataset and add it to your project |
| **Templates** | Ready-made project layouts |
| **Toggle Dashboard** | Shows or hides the tiles in your current project |
| **All Projects** | Your list of projects and project groups |
| **Alerts** | Planning alerts you've set up |
| **Site Board** | Sites you've shortlisted |
| **Bespoke Data Request** | Ask REalyse for data that isn't in the platform |
| **Help** | Help and feedback |
| **Account** | Your plan, subscription and export credits |

Some areas, such as **Alerts** and **Site Board**, depend on your plan.
