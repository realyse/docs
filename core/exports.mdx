## Export a project

Open the project, click the project menu and choose **Export**. Then pick a format:

| Option | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Export to Word** | A Word document of your project, ready to edit for a report |
| **Export to PDF** | A PDF of your project (not available on every account) |
| **Export to CSV** | The project's data as CSV |
| **Export to Excel** | The project's data as an Excel workbook |

## Export a single tile

To save one chart or map as an image, use **Export as PNG** on the tile.

## Export alerts and Site Board

- On the **Alerts** page, open an alert's menu and choose **Export CSV** or **Export Excel**.
- On the **Site Board**, choose **Export to CSV** or **Export to Excel**. See [Site Board](/core/site-board).

## Export credits

Some exports use export credits. To see how they work, open the **How Export Credits work** guide in the app.

You can check your balance and buy more in your **Account** panel, under **Export Credits**. See [Account and subscription](/core/account).
