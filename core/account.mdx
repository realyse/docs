Click **Account** at the bottom of the sidebar to open your account panel.

## What the account panel shows

- Your name and email address
- **Plan Selected:** your current plan, such as **Lite**, **Basic** or **Enterprise**
- **Subscription Period:** your **Renewal date**, or **Pending cancellation** if you've cancelled
- **Export Credits:** your **Current Balance**

Enterprise accounts are managed by REalyse, so the sidebar shows **Logout** instead of **Account**. Contact your account manager about plan changes.

## Manage your subscription

Click **Manage Subscription** in the account panel to change or cancel your subscription.

## Buy export credits

Click **Buy credits** in the account panel. [Exports](/core/exports) explains which exports use credits.

## Sign out

Click **Log out** in the account panel.

## Password and sign-in

To change your password, sign out and use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. See [Get started](/core/getting-started#reset-your-password).

## Get help

- Click **Help** in the sidebar to contact us from inside the app.
- Email [support@realyse.com](mailto:support@realyse.com).
