## Datasets

Open **Explore** in the sidebar to choose a dataset.

| Dataset | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Comparables** | Sold and asking prices, rents and land transactions, with filters such as bedrooms, property type and EPC rating |
| **Planning** | Individual planning applications and the planning pipeline for an area |
| **Land & Ownership** | HM Land Registry titles, boundaries and owners |
| **Demographics** | Population and household data, crime, broadband, ethnicity, homelessness and more |
| **Points of Interest** | Nearby schools, transport, shops and other amenities |
| **Markets** | Market indicators over time, such as prices, rents and yields |
| **Policies** | Policy rates and local planning policy for an area |
| **Agent Comparison** | How estate agents compare on market share |
| **Overview** | A summary of an area across several datasets |

The datasets available to you depend on your plan. If you need data that isn't here, click **Bespoke Data Request** in the sidebar.

## Choose a location

You can search for an area or draw one on the map.

### Search

Use the **Search by** switch to choose what you're searching for:

- **Postcode**
- **Local Authority**
- **Title Number**
- **Planning Ref Number**
- **London Transport Zones**

### Draw on the map

- **Draw Polygon** to outline any shape
- **Draw Circle** to choose a radius around a point
- **Select location on map** to click an area on the map

### Reuse locations

Open **Locations** on the map. The picker has three tabs: **Project Locations** for areas already used in this project, **Favourites** for areas you've saved, and **Global**.

### Save a favourite

A favourite is a copy of a project location, so you can use the same area in another project.

Search for the area, or draw it. When it shows as a draft, click **Save**. It then sits under **Project Locations**.

![Draft location ready to save](/images/core/favourite-location-save.jpg)

Click the star beside that location.

![Project location with a star](/images/core/favourite-location-star.jpg)

The star fills in, and Core shows **Location successfully favourited.**

![Location successfully favourited](/images/core/favourite-location-saved.jpg)

Open the **Favourites** tab to see it. You can rename it with the pencil, or delete it with the bin. Hover the row and click **Use Location** to add that area to the project you have open.

![Favourites tab with the saved location](/images/core/favourite-location-list.jpg)

If the star is already filled, that location is a favourite. The tooltip says **Location already favourited**.

## Choose a date range

Pick a preset under **Previous**: **3 Months**, **6 Months**, **12 Months**, **18 Months**, **2 Years** or **5 Years**. Longer ranges may depend on your plan.

## Alerts

You can set an alert on a planning application to hear about changes. Open the application's record and click **Create Alert**.

Open **Alerts** in the sidebar to see your alerts, filtered by **All**, **New Applications** or **Changed Applications**. You can export an alert's results to CSV or Excel, or delete it.

## Site Board

Use **Site Board** to keep a shortlist of sites you're interested in. You can export the board to CSV or Excel.
