## Sign in

Go to [app.realyse.com](https://app.realyse.com). On the **Sign in to REalyse** page, enter your email and password and click **Sign in**.

If your organisation uses Google or Microsoft accounts, you may also see **Continue with Google** and **Continue with Microsoft**.

Some accounts need a code sent by text message. If you're asked for a **Code from sms**, enter the code from your phone and click **Sign in** again.

## First time signing in

If REalyse has invited you, your invitation email has a link to set your password.

1. Open the link and choose a password on the **Set a Password** page.
2. Fill in **Complete profile set up** with your name, mobile number, company name and company type.

## Reset your password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter your email address and click **Reset**.
3. Open the link in the email we send you and choose a new password.

## Build your first project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Open **All Projects** in the sidebar and start a new project, or open the project menu and choose **New project**. You can also start from a layout in **Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose some data">
    Click **Explore** in the sidebar and pick a dataset, such as **Comparables** or **Planning**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the location and dates">
    Search for a postcode or local authority, or draw an area on the map. Then choose a date range, such as the last 12 months.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a visualisation">
    Choose how to show the data, for example as a map, line chart or table, and add it to your project as a tile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Repeat for each dataset you want to compare. [Projects](/core/projects) covers organising, copying and sharing project layouts.

<Tip>
  Each REalyse account is for one person. If your account is used on another device at the same time, you'll see a warning.
</Tip>
